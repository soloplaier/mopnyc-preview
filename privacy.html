What we collect

When you book or contact us we collect your name, email, phone number, address, access notes and booking details. Payments are processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.

How we use it

To schedule and complete your cleaning, send confirmations and reminders by email and text, take payment, ask for feedback, and improve our service. Your address and access notes are shared only with the cleaner assigned to your job.

Advertising and analytics

We use cookies and the Meta pixel to measure our ads and show relevant ads to visitors. You can opt out through your browser or ad settings.

Messages

By booking, you agree to receive transactional texts and emails about your booking. Reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time.
