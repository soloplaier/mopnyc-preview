FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I choose my cleaner?

Yes. You see each cleaner's profile and open times when you book, and you can keep the same person every time.

What if my cleaner isn't available?

We text you first. You can pick another cleaner from the team or choose a different time. Nobody is sent without your OK.

When do I pay?

Your card is saved when you book and charged only after your clean is finished.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients leave a key with the doorman or use a lockbox. Add access notes when you book.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before, using the link in your confirmation. Inside 24 hours, or if we can't get in, a 50% fee applies.

What if I'm not happy?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and fix it, free.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes, all supplies and equipment. Prefer your own products? Add a note when you book.

Which areas do you cover?

Manhattan · Brooklyn · Queens. Enter your zip code when booking to confirm.
