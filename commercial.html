How contracts work

Simple from first visit to invoice.

  1. Walkthrough. We visit the site and agree the scope with you.
  2. Proposal in 48 hours. A fixed price per visit or per month, with the checklist attached.
  3. Start date. A dedicated crew and one point of contact.
  4. Every visit. Checked against the checklist with a photo report.
  5. One invoice. Monthly, with no surprise charges.
