Before you book

When do I pay?

Your card is saved when you book and charged only after your clean is finished.

How do I get the cleaner in?

Be home, leave a key with the doorman, or use a lockbox. Add access notes when you book.

Can I keep the same cleaner?

Yes. Pick them from the team when you book, and choose them again next time.

What's the cancellation policy?

Free up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours, or if we can't get in, a 50% fee applies.

What if something's not right?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and fix it, free.
